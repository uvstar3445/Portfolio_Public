A little about me

Engineering, data, and systems

I’m Yuvraj, an Engineering Systems and Computing student at the University of Guelph. I expect to graduate in April 2027.

My interests sit between data analysis and the systems behind it. I’ve compared housing-price prediction models in Python, built Power BI reports, and worked on embedded hardware–software integration through my coursework.

During my eight-month co-op at Definity, I supported technology workflows, built QA test profiles, and helped with reporting and documentation alongside development and QA teams. Before that, I worked at Staples, troubleshooting technical issues and explaining solutions to customers.

Outside coursework, I maintain a home server using Proxmox, Linux, and Docker. It gives me a practical way to learn about services, storage, networking, and troubleshooting when something stops working.
